Pick a note on the left. These are the vaults you linked to your account — the same documents Plume reads on your desktop, rendered the same way.

A browser cannot reach the folders on your disk, so local-only vaults and loose folders are not here. To read one of those in a browser, link it to your account from the desktop app: Plume Vault → Link to my account. Nothing is uploaded until you do.
